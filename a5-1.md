## Test Ideas – Password Validation

1. Verify minimum password length: Enter a password with exactly 8 characters, including at least one digit and one uppercase letter. Verify that it is accepted.
2. Verify maximum password length: Enter a password with exactly 20 characters, including at least one digit and one uppercase letter. Verify that it is accepted.
3. Verify password below minimum length: Enter a password with 7 characters, including at least one digit and one uppercase letter. Verify that it is rejected.
4. Verify password above maximum length: Enter a password with 21 characters, including at least one digit and one uppercase letter. Verify that it is rejected.
5. Verify password requires at least one digit: Enter a password with 8–20 characters containing at least one uppercase letter but no digits. Verify that it is rejected.
6. Verify password requires at least one uppercase letter: Enter a password with 8–20 characters containing at least one digit but no uppercase letters. Verify that it is rejected.
